## Reading the table

- An edge is claimed only when the whole 95% CI is above zero. Otherwise the row says *no edge* and RoR is 100%.
- Penetration is the dominant factor. Confirm it by counting how many cards are dealt before the shuffle over several shoes, then rerun with that value.
- "Wong out" (watching low counts without betting) is the strongest tool *if* the table lets you sit out without a penalty or being removed. Many live-dealer tables limit seats or skipped rounds. Verify this.
- EV / hour depends on the pace assumption. Multiply by your unit size to get dollars per hour.
- RoR uses the per-round mean and SD from the simulation. It ignores table limits and bet-sizing errors, so it is optimistic.
